I am a Ph.D. student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, supervised by Professor [Hong-Bin Shen](https://sais.sjtu.edu.cn/faculty/shenhongbin.html)@[Pattern Recognition and Bioinformatics Group](http://www.csbio.sjtu.edu.cn/).

My research interests lie in developing deep learning models for **AI for Science (AI4S)**.
I aim to build data-centric and scalable AI systems that leverage large-scale biological data to address fundamental challenges in computational biology and drug discovery.

My current research focuses on:

- Protein–ligand interaction modeling
- AI-driven scoring functions for molecular docking
- Graph neural networks for drug discovery

I worked on deep learning approaches for biological and chemical problems.
I am particularly interested in designing models that integrate **sequence, structure, molecular graphs, and biochemical knowledge** to improve the understanding and generation of functional biomolecules.

I am open to academic collaborations and discussions related to AI4S, computational biology, and deep learning.
